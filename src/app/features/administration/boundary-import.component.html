Upload your estate survey as a GeoJSON FeatureCollection, then link each polygon to an estate, division or field. Coordinates must be WGS84 longitude / latitude. Up to 100 polygons and 1 MiB per file.

This adds new boundaries. Records that already have geometry are excluded. No boundaries, areas or coordinates are generated for you. KML/shapefiles must first be exported to WGS84 GeoJSON in your GIS software.
